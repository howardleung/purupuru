# Retailer logo asset policy

Retailer marks are optional presentation assets. Offer identity and links remain data-driven, and accessible linked retailer text is the required fallback. The registry in `apps/web/lib/retailer-logos.ts` is keyed by stable `Retailer.sourceKey`; display-name matching is not permitted.

Only repository-hosted artwork with reviewed provenance and sufficiently clear reuse terms may be added. Do not hotlink logos, reconstruct marks, or copy storefront artwork without an applicable grant. A mark must identify the exact retailer represented by the offer rather than a related manufacturer or parent brand.

Before adding an asset, record:

- the exact source URL and retrieval date;
- the applicable permission or license;
- dimensions and file format;
- required attribution, if any;
- the retailer source key it represents.

No retailer logo files are currently included. Offers intentionally render accessible text fallbacks until a permission review supports a local asset. Trademark review remains separate from copyright provenance.
